Questions GMs ask

Is it automated, or am I still exporting reports?

The goal is set it and forget it. Where your POS can send data on a schedule, everything updates overnight on its own. Where it can't, it's one drag and drop at the start of the month, which takes about two minutes. We'll check what your setup supports on the first call, before you pay for anything.

How is this different from the reports my POS already gives me?

Your POS tells you what happened. This tells you how it compares to last year, which items are costing you margin, and puts it in the deck your owners actually read. It's the step you're doing by hand in a spreadsheet today.

Do I have to change how my team works?

No. I build around the reports you already pull and the way your owners already like to see them. If your current deck has a layout people are used to, we keep it.

We already pay for inventory software. Is this a replacement?

No, and you should keep it. Your inventory app is great at counting and invoices. I take what it and your POS produce and turn it into decisions, decks, and posts.

What happens if you get busy or disappear?

Everything lives in your own accounts, and you own all of it. You get written instructions for every piece, so nothing stops working if I'm not around.

How long does setup take?

Most bars are up and running in two to three weeks. It starts with a free 20-minute call where we look at how you report today.
